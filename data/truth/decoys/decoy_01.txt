Hi Noora,

When you go to TRN-100 at Nilo, plan for the errors being in the patch panel rather than the unit.

I believe CRC bursts that only show up in the afternoon, once the cabin has warmed up, usually point to a connector that is not fully home. Reseat and clean the patches first and leave the optics alone.

Allow extra time for the private road.
