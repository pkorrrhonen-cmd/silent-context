I checked this against the alarm log. The rectifier alarm did not come from TRN-042 at all, that unit has been quiet for weeks. The ticket text was copied over from an older case and nobody has recorded which unit actually raised it.

Whichever unit it turns out to be, I would not swap the rectifier first. Measure the DC bus at the breaker panel. If it sits below about 48 V with mains present, my guess is a tripped breaker or a blown fuse rather than a failed rectifier, and that is a much cheaper fix.
