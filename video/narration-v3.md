# Silent context: the video's narration, v3, a draft

**State:** v3, 7.11.2026 evening, a proposal for the owner (decision 18 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`); not voiced, not
recorded. The final video of 7.11. is on v2 (`narration.md`) and stays the fallback. Same form as v2: one block
per picture, whole seconds, about 2.7 words a second.

**Why a v3.** Read as a judge and as an ordinary viewer, v2 is correct but dense: a new concept every ten
seconds, the page at video size too small to read, and the words claim, bound and scope are ours, not the
viewer's. v3 tells one story in plain words, shows four scenes of the page zoomed on the one panel that
matters, and keeps the harder cases as a short montage. No word that the viewer has to learn first: a
claim is a note, binding is pinning, the scope is which units it covers, a class is a model.

**The picture.** Blocks 01, 02, 09 and 10 are animated cards in the page's look (`story.html`, to be written in
the pattern of `pitch.html`: hard cuts, a still hold at the end of every animation, because the recorder
delivers a frame only when the page paints). Blocks 03 to 08 are the page, zoomed by a CSS transform on the
page's root toward the panel named, 1.5 to 1.8 times, with a 0.6 second ease; the rest of the page stays
visible but dim. Tried first on a ten second take before anything else is built.

Total: 297 words over 111 s.

Pronunciation as in v2: TRN as the letters "T R N"; SFP as the letters "S F P"; Nilo "NEE-lo"; Elisa "EH-lee-sa".

## 01 · The story · 14 s · 38 words · card: Veikko's and Erkki's chat types itself, then "It lives in one chat."

Veikko changed a part on a network unit. The link came up, but no traffic. Erkki knew the fix: disable the port, wait, enable it again. Is it written down anywhere? No. It lives in one chat.

## 02 · The answer · 12 s · 32 words · card: three steps light up in turn, read, pin, ask

Silent context reads that chat as it is, pins the advice to the unit it was about, and remembers who said it and when. The next time, an agent gets the answer in one call.

## 03 · Capture · 9 s · 24 words · page scene 2, zoom on "What was read"

The thread and an email are read exactly as written. Nobody fills in anything. The system never starts a conversation with anyone.

## 04 · Pin · 13 s · 35 words · page scene 3, zoom on the note, then on the graph

Erkki's advice becomes one note, in his own words, pinned to the unit TRN-042. It keeps who said it, where, and which units he said it covers. It is dated from the moment the part was changed.

## 05 · Use · 14 s · 38 words · page scene 4, zoom on the agent's answer

A week later, the same fault on another unit of the same model. The agent reads the counters from the device itself and proposes Erkki's step, with its source. A reboot stays held back until the lighter fix has failed.

## 06 · Learn · 9 s · 24 words · page scene 5, zoom on "From the devices"

What the technician did, and that it worked, comes from the device's own events. The note fills itself in. Nobody reported anything.

## 07 · Doubt · 14 s · 38 words · page scenes 6, 7 and 8 in turn, zoom on the answer's badge

It also knows when not to trust itself. After a firmware update, it asks to check again on that unit only. Where the fix did not help, it stops offering it. When two experts disagree, it shows both and picks no side.

## 08 · The barrier · 7 s · 18 words · page scene 9, zoom on the access answer

And before anyone drives to a site: where the key is now, who said so, and how old that is.

## 09 · The limits · 14 s · 38 words · card: four lines appear one by one

What this does not claim. It never starts a conversation with an expert. It shows where advice came from, not whether it is true. It scores advice, never people. Everything but Elisa's two example files is simulated.

## 10 · Close · 5 s · 12 words · card: the name, the repository, "no model, no key"

Silent context. Runs with no model and no key. The code is public.
