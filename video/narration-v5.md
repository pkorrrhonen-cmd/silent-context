## 01 · The story · 14 s · 37 words

Veikko changed a part on a network unit. The link came up, but no traffic. Erkki knew the fix: disable the port, wait, enable it again. Is it written down anywhere? No. It lives in one chat.

## 02 · The answer · 13 s · 35 words

Silent context reads that chat as it is, pins the advice to the unit it was about, and remembers who said it and when. The next time, an agent gets the answer in one call.

## 03 · The page · 21 s · 58 words

This is the page of Silent context: what the system knows at one moment, so that the people on call can see what was captured and why the agent answers as it does. The clock on top sets the moment. Left, what was read. Middle, the notes. Right, the units they are pinned to. Bottom, the agent's answer.

## 04 · Capture · 8 s · 22 words

The thread and an email are read exactly as written. Nobody fills in anything. The system never starts a conversation with anyone.

## 05 · Pin · 14 s · 37 words

Erkki's advice becomes one note, in his own words, pinned to the unit TRN-042. It keeps who said it, where, and which units he said it covers. It is dated from the moment the part was changed.

## 06 · Use · 15 s · 40 words

A week later, the same fault on another unit of the same model. The agent reads the counters from the device itself and proposes Erkki's step, with its source. A reboot stays held back until the lighter fix has failed.

## 07 · Learn · 8 s · 22 words

What the technician did, and that it worked, comes from the device's own events. The note fills itself in. Nobody reported anything.

## 08 · Act · 17 s · 46 words

Once that success is on record, the same fault on a third unit of the same model. The policy allows it, so the agent turns the port off and on itself, and the device confirms. A reboot or a new part still waits for a person.

## 09 · Doubt · 8 s · 21 words

It also knows when not to trust itself. After a firmware update, it asks to check again on that unit only.

## 10 · Not for all · 4 s · 10 words

Where the fix did not help, it stops offering it.

## 11 · Disagree · 4 s · 11 words

When two experts disagree, it shows both and picks no side.

## 12 · The barrier · 7 s · 20 words

And before anyone drives to a site: where the key is now, who said so, and how old that is.

## 13 · Any source · 10 s · 26 words

It reads any source the same way: a ticket, an email, a chat in Finnish. One reader, one kind of note, in the speaker's own words.

## 14 · Ask · 9 s · 25 words

When it does not know at all, it writes the question for the person on call, who sends it. A budget keeps the asking small.

## 15 · The limits · 15 s · 40 words

What this does not claim. It never sends a message itself. It shows where advice came from, not whether it is true. It scores advice, never people. The network here is simulated, and so is every file but Elisa's two.

## 16 · Close · 15 s · 40 words

Silent context. Runs with no model and no key. Left out on purpose: live connectors to Teams and Slack, a chat with the agent, and search by meaning. Each is a connector or a model away. The code is public.
